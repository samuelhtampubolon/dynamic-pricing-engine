    02 · Econometrics

    Econometric Elasticity Studio

    365 days of synthetic demand per SKU, fitted with log-log OLS plus day-of-week fixed effects. Full inference, not just a slope.

    Coefficient table

    TermEstimateSEt95% CITrue

    Log-log demand curve

    Daily ln Q adjusted for day-of-week and competitor price, against ln P. The slope is ε.

    Profit optimisation curve

    Gross profit per day with Duan-smeared demand. P* sits where marginal revenue equals marginal cost.

    Lerner markup multiplier

    P*/c = ε/(1+ε). Demand near unit elasticity makes the optimal markup explode, which is exactly why the MSRP ceiling exists.

    03 · Online experimentation

    Multi-Armed Bandit Experimentation Arena

    Three policies race on the same SKU. Each day is one round; demand is Poisson from the fitted curve; reward is gross profit.

    Cumulative pseudo-regret

    RT = Σ (μ* − μat). Flattening curves mean the policy has found the best price.

    Cumulative profit vs. static price

    Realised gross profit minus the static baseline's realised profit.

    Arm allocation

    Rounds spent on each candidate price. Arms are clamped into the guardrail envelope before the experiment starts.

    Why a robust z-score: the masking attack

    Inject a coordinated batch of poisoned volume events. The classic mean/σ z-score lets the batch inflate σ and hide itself; the median/MAD gate does not.
